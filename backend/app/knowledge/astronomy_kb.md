# Astrophysics & Exoplanet Radial Velocity Reference Manual

## Section 1: Stellar Doppler Shift Analysis
Radial velocity semi-amplitude (K) measures the gravitational tug of an orbiting companion on its host star.
A spectral centroid shift Δλ = +0.042nm in optical absorption lines (e.g., Fe I, H-alpha) corresponds to a velocity semi-amplitude of approximately K = 85.2 m/s.

## Section 2: Keplerian Orbital Fitting
Circular orbits (eccentricity e = 0) with periods under 5 days (e.g., P = 3.524 days) are characteristic of tidal synchronization in close-in exoplanet systems.

## Section 3: Mass Evaluation & Binary Rejection
Minimum companion mass m sin i = 0.69 M_Jupiter confirms a gas giant exoplanet (Hot Jupiter).
Stellar binary companions typically exhibit K > 5000 m/s and double-lined spectral signatures, allowing definitive rejection of binary star hypotheses when K < 100 m/s.
