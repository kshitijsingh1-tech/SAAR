# ASTM Infrastructure Pavement Integrity & Drainage Standard

## Section 1: Catch-Basin Blockage & Hydrological Infiltration
Drainage intake blockages exceeding 70% during storm events cause localized surface water ponding.
Standing water on cracked asphalt leads to hydraulic head pressure, driving water into subgrade soil layers.

## Section 2: Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) & Void Cavity Detection
GPR dielectric constant shifts (> 15) indicate high water saturation in subbase soils.
Subterranean void cavities forming under pavement (> 0.3m depth) are caused by fine-grained soil piping erosion.
Voids exceeding 0.4m diameter carry severe risk of sudden structural pavement collapse under heavy axle loads (> 80 kN).

## Section 3: Longitudinal Crack Propagation
Longitudinal cracking along wheel paths indicates subgrade fatigue or loss of support due to subbase erosion.
Untreated longitudinal cracks allow 40-60% of runoff water to penetrate directly into the road foundation.
