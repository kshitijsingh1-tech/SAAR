# Badminton Biomechanics & Energy Expenditure Knowledge Base

## 1. Energy Expenditure & Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET)

Badminton is a high-intensity intermittent racket sport characterized by rapid multidirectional lunges, jumps, short accelerations, and high-frequency stroke production interspersed with brief recovery intervals.

### Compendium of Physical Activities Reference Codes
According to the standardized *Compendium of Physical Activities* (Ainsworth et al., 1993, 2000, 2011; Herrmann et al., 2024 update):
- **Code 15050**: Badminton, social singles and doubles, general play: **5.5 METs**
- **Code 15040**: Badminton, competitive match play, singles tournament: **7.0 METs**
- **Code 15041**: Badminton, high-intensity elite match play / continuous fast-tempo rally: **8.5 METs**

The literature consensus establishes a competitive badminton match play range of **5.5 to 8.5 METs**, with **7.0 METs** representing the established normative baseline for active match play (e.g., Phomsoupha & Laffaye, "The Science of Badminton: Game Characteristics, Anthropometry, Physiology, Visual Fitness and Biomechanics", *Sports Medicine*, 2015; Cabello Manrique & González-Badillo, 2003).

### Metabolic Calculation Formulation
Gross energy expenditure is calculated using the established American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) metabolic equation:
$$\text{EE (kcal)} = \text{MET} \times 3.5 \times \frac{\text{Body Mass (kg)}}{200} \times \text{Duration (minutes)}$$

Where:
- $1\text{ MET} \equiv 3.5\,\text{mL } \text{O}_2 \cdot \text{kg}^{-1} \cdot \text{min}^{-1} \approx 0.0175\,\text{kcal} \cdot \text{kg}^{-1} \cdot \text{min}^{-1} = \frac{3.5}{200}\,\text{kcal} \cdot \text{kg}^{-1} \cdot \text{min}^{-1}$.
- Equivalently: $\text{EE (kcal)} = \text{MET} \times \text{Body Mass (kg)} \times \left(\frac{\text{Duration (min)}}{60}\right) \times 1.05$.

### Population-Average Reference Mass
When individual body mass is not provided by the athlete during intake:
- Standard reference adult mass: **70.0 kg** (Compendium standard adult reference weight; Ainsworth et al., 2011).
- Any calculation utilizing this default must be explicitly designated as **`population_average_generalized`** rather than **`personalized`**.

### Movement Intensity Modulation
Optical tracking of player displacement and court coverage modulates the effective MET value within the empirical 5.5 – 8.5 range:
- **Low Movement Tempo** (average speed $< 0.8\,\text{m/s}$ or stationary drill): $\text{MET} = 5.5$
- **Standard Competitive Tempo** ($0.8\,\text{m/s} \le \text{speed} < 1.4\,\text{m/s}$): $\text{MET} = 7.0$
- **High-Intensity Rally Tempo** (average speed $\ge 1.4\,\text{m/s}$ or court coverage $\ge 50\%$): $\text{MET} = 8.5$

## 2. Non-Medical & Non-Nutritional Disclaimer
Energy expenditure figures generated by this engine represent gross physical estimates derived from empirical population-average kinematic and metabolic models. They are intended strictly for athletic training load monitoring, rally tempo quantification, and sports performance tracking. They do not constitute clinical metabolic assessment, dietary or nutritional prescription, caloric restriction guidance, or medical diagnosis/treatment for metabolic or endocrine conditions.
